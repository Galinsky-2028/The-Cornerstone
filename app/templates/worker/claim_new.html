You are eligible if the work a certified event targeted was your principal livelihood (more than half of your earned income, or at least 1,000 hours, in a representative 12 months before the harm began) and you lost it, or a substantial part of it, during the certified causal period. File within two years of certification or of your separation, whichever is later. Labor must decide within 90 days.
