Cornerstone Associations

Section 4. Certified workers form an association, which Labor charters. Enrollment then stays open for 180 days. Each certified claim is one founding unit: it cannot be bought, sold, pledged or transferred, and every member's vote counts the same. A roll needs between 2 and 2,500 units. A worker whose claim is still under review may elect an enrolling association as a cohort; the claim becomes a unit only if certified before the roll closes.
